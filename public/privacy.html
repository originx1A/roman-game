Roman's Game. Draft updated October 18, 2026.

Roman is a fictional character. This game is for a general audience, including children. There is no advertising and no loot box. Prices on a payment page are the real prices.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress in this device's local storage (the browser or the app's localStorage). That includes:

  • Coins, hints, shields, spins, badges, and scores
  • Boards you have started but not finished
  • Sound, voice, and reduce-motion settings
  • Challenge links you created on this device
  • A display name, if you type one. On a guest save it stays on this device.
  • An optional friend email you type for a challenge. That stays on this device with your challenges. It is not uploaded by the game.
  • After a coin purchase, an id for that purchase is saved on this device so the same purchase is not counted twice. In the apps that id comes from the store. On the website it is the checkout id.

Playing as a guest keeps that save on this device. Deleting the app, or clearing the site data, deletes the guest save.

Signed-in play

You can keep playing without an account. If you sign in, the game emails you a link. There is no password. Your email, the leaderboard name you pick, and the scores you finish are saved to your account so they can follow you to another device. Guest progress already on this device moves into the account. It is not wiped. Coins and buddies earned in play are copied onto this device's account snapshot. A full cross-device copy of earned coins is not turned on yet.

Anonymous play counts (added September 29, 2026)

The website version counts how the game is used, without knowing who you are. When the game opens, a level is cleared (with its board size and time), you come back on another day, or you tap 👍/👎 (with a short note if you write one), the game sends that with a random number made once on your device. The server adds a rough place (city, region, country) from the request and the time. We do not collect your name, email, exact location or IP address, and the game never asks for location permission. You can switch this off in Save & settings. The iPhone and Android apps do not send it.

Sharing

If you share a score or a challenge, the game builds a link. The link can include your display name, the email you saved on this device, your message, and your score. Copy, Messages, Mail, or another app you choose then handles that link. The game itself does not send the message.

Sound and voice

Sound effects and Roman's voice clips are files inside the app. They play on your device. When voice comments are on, the game also uses your device's built-in text-to-speech (the system speech feature). That speech stays on the device. The game does not record your microphone.

Coin purchases

Buying coins requires a sign-in. The account holder sets a 4-digit purchase PIN the first time, and types it before every payment page. The game stores a scrambled copy of that PIN on the server, not the digits themselves. A reset link can be emailed to the sign-in address. Five wrong tries lock purchases for 15 minutes. The website also stops new coin purchases for an account after CA$50 in a rolling week. Stripe hosts the payment page. That page can offer Apple Pay or Google Pay, which use the phone's Face ID or fingerprint, or a card typed in for that purchase. The game does not save the card for a one-tap buy later, and it does not see or store the card number. After you pay, the site asks Stripe whether that payment succeeded, then adds the coins. A purchase record (the pack, the amount, the time, and a short reference, not your card) is stored on the game server and tied to the signed-in account so the same payment is not counted twice.

In the iPhone and Android apps, buying coins uses Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), not the website checkout. The app uses the capacitor-plugin-cdv-purchase plugin to talk to StoreKit on iPhone and Google Play Billing on Android.

This version does not send purchase receipts to the developer and does not use a third-party receipt checker. Apple or Google still receive the purchase as part of their normal checkout. The plugin can send device details (such as operating system and model) to a receipt checker if one is turned on. It is not turned on in this version.

Coins are added only after the store finishes a successful purchase. If you cancel, if the payment is still pending, or if it fails, no coins are added. Coin packs are consumable: once a purchase is finished, the store does not give those coins back through “Restore”. Restore only finishes a payment that was charged but not yet added.

Analytics

The anonymous play counts above are first-party. You can switch them off in Save & settings. Google Analytics is not loaded unless you turn it on in that same screen. It is off by default. When it is on, the site uses a Google Analytics 4 tag with ads personalization turned off. Those events do not include your name, email, or account id. The iPhone and Android apps do not load it.

What this game does not do

  • No ads
  • No sale of personal information
  • No loot boxes or random paid rewards
  • No Google Analytics unless you opt in

Children

The game is made for a general audience, including children. It does not knowingly collect information for an advertising profile. Guests can play every mode. Buying coins needs a sign-in and the purchase PIN. A parent can also use the store's Ask to Buy / family payment approval. Coins are added only when the payment finishes.
